# {shop_name}: how we use your messages

**Draft, not legally reviewed.** Template version `customer-notice-2026-10-18-draft1`. Each shop's page at
`/{slug}/privacy` is generated from this template and the shop's details. To be reviewed by a lawyer before the
public launch (BETA_10 §14).

---

When you message **{shop_name}** on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram or by phone, an assistant may answer
for the shop. The assistant is provided by {product_name}.

## Who is responsible

**{shop_name}** decides how your messages and orders are used. Contact: {shop_contact}.

{product_name} runs the assistant for {shop_name} and processes your data only on the shop's behalf.

## What is used, and why

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and number or account name on the channel you use | To know it is you, and reply to you |
| Your messages, photos and voice notes | To answer you and take your orders |
| Your orders, delivery details and payments | To deliver what you ordered and keep the shop's accounts |
| Things you tell the shop you prefer (for example a usual order, or "eggless") | To serve you faster next time. You can ask what is remembered, and ask to forget it |
| Records of calls, if you call | To help with what you called about |

Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

## Messages you do not want

If you do not want messages from {shop_name}, say so in your own words, for example "please stop messaging me".
You can change your mind the same way.

## How long it is kept

Messages and details: up to 24 months after you last contact the shop. Orders: 8 years for the shop's accounts,
with your name and number removed after 24 months without contact. Some records are kept for one year, as the law
requires.

## Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, or delete it. Just ask in the chat (for example "delete my data" or "what
do you have about me?"), or contact {shop_name} at {shop_contact}. You get an answer within **30 days**.

If you are not satisfied, you can contact {product_name}'s grievance officer at {privacy_email}, and then the
Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

If you are under 18, please do not share personal details. Tell the assistant, and a person from the shop will
help you.
