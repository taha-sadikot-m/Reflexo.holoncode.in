4.2 **On your instructions only.** We process your customers' data only to provide {product_name} to you, and only as

- every business's data kept apart from every other business's;
- credentials (your tokens and keys) stored encrypted, with every use logged;

4.6 **Rights requests.** When a customer asks to see, correct or delete their data, {product_name} records the request

content. You can opt out at any time in Settings.

4.9 **Audit.** On request, we give you the information you reasonably need to check that we keep these promises.
